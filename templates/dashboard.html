    Strict Tri-State Conservation Categorization

    ForestSentinel separates taxonomic presence from threat evaluation. When IUCN verification is absent or pending, the system never defaults to "unthreatened" or calculates unverified exposure scores:

    01 Pending IUCN Verification

    Assigned when IUCN Red List verification has not yet run or is pending. Species exposure score is not calculated.

    02 Verified Not Threatened

    Assigned only when IUCN verification succeeds and the species is categorized as Least Concern (LC) or Near Threatened (NT).

    03 Verified Threatened

    Assigned only when IUCN verification confirms Vulnerable (VU), Endangered (EN), or Critically Endangered (CR) status.

    ⚠️

    Scientific notice: retrospective analytical ranking of recorded NASA FIRMS thermal anomalies — not a wildfire-prediction model, not an official emergency warning, and never a claim of confirmed animal mortality.
