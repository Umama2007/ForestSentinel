Scientific Rigor & Conservation Methodology

IUCN & GBIF Standards

Strict Tri-State Conservation Categorization

To prevent misinterpretation, ForestSentinel explicitly separates taxonomic presence from threat evaluation. When IUCN verification is absent or pending, the system prohibits defaulting to unthreatened or calculating unverified exposure scores:

1. Pending IUCN Verification Assigned when IUCN Red List verification has not yet run or is pending. Species exposure score is not calculated. (23 species in current run)
2. Verified Not Threatened Assigned only when IUCN verification succeeds and the species is categorized as Least Concern (LC) or Near Threatened (NT).
3. Verified Threatened Assigned only when IUCN verification confirms Vulnerable (VU), Endangered (EN), or Critically Endangered (CR) status.
